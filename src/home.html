Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what property owners ask us most often

How much does a public adjuster cost?
Public-adjuster compensation is governed by Florida law and the written engagement agreement. The permitted fee depends on the type and timing of the claim, prior payments, any applicable emergency declaration, and the services requested. We explain the applicable fee in writing before an engagement is signed.
How long does an insurance claim take?
Timing varies with the policy, scope of damage, available documentation, inspections, carrier response, and whether a dispute develops. We set expectations after reviewing the facts and keep clients informed as the claim progresses.
Can I switch from another adjuster or attorney?
Yes. You have the right to change representation on your claim. We'll review your existing contract and help coordinate the transition.
My claim was denied. Can you still help?
Possibly. We review the denial, policy, damage documentation, deadlines, and prior submissions to determine whether additional investigation or a supplemental presentation may be appropriate. Results depend on the facts and policy terms.
Is the consultation really free?
Yes. We'll review your policy, inspect the damage, and tell you honestly whether you have a claim worth pursuing — at no cost and with no obligation.
What types of damage do you handle?
Hurricane and wind damage, flood, fire and smoke, water damage from plumbing failures, theft and vandalism, mold, and other covered property losses for both residential and commercial properties.

What a Well-Prepared Claim Includes

Every loss is different. Our work focuses on building a clear, traceable record instead of promising a particular outcome.

Damage Documentation

Scope tied to evidence

Photographs, measurements, moisture data, reports, and observations organized around the specific components in dispute.

Estimate Review

Transparent repair pricing

Line-item estimates reviewed for quantities, labor, materials, repair methodology, code items, and documented property conditions.

Policy & Claim Record

Issues clearly organized

Relevant policy provisions, carrier communications, inspections, estimates, and deadlines assembled into a coherent claim presentation.

Communication

Consistent client updates

A defined point of contact, documented requests, follow-up on outstanding items, and clear explanations of material developments.
