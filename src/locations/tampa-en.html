Tampa Bay's flat geography and bay-front exposure created a wake-up call during recent hurricane seasons. From South Tampa to Westchase and Brandon to St. Petersburg, we help property owners cut through carrier delay tactics.

Claim Types We Handle in Tampa

Local exposure drives local strategy. These are the losses we see most often in Hillsborough County.

Hurricane Wind & Surge

Helene and Milton exposed Tampa Bay's surge vulnerability. Combined wind and flood claims require precise causation documentation to recover full policy benefits.

Sinkhole & Structural

Hillsborough and Pasco counties remain Florida's sinkhole belt. Catastrophic ground cover collapse claims demand engineering reports and aggressive carrier follow-through.

Water & Plumbing

South Tampa's older bungalows and Hyde Park's historic homes carry aging plumbing. Sudden discharge events frequently flood multiple rooms and contents.

Commercial Property

Tampa's industrial corridors, retail centers, and office parks generate complex business-interruption and building claims that demand professional adjusting.

Tampa Claim FAQ

Do you serve all of Hillsborough County?
Yes. We work claims throughout Hillsborough County including Brandon, Carrollwood, Channelside, Davis Islands, Hyde Park, Lutz, New Tampa, Riverview, Seminole Heights, South Tampa, St. Petersburg, Tampa Palms, Town 'N' Country, Westchase, Ybor City.
How much does a public adjuster cost in Tampa?
We work on contingency — no upfront fee. Our percentage is regulated by Florida statute and is capped lower in declared catastrophe events.
My carrier already paid. Can you still help?
Often, yes. Florida law allows supplemental claims when additional damage is discovered or when the original payment did not capture the full scope of loss.
How long do I have to file a claim?
Florida statutes set strict deadlines that have shortened in recent years. Don't assume — call us and we'll confirm the deadline that applies to your loss.
Parlez-vous français?
Oui. We handle claims in both English and French for property owners and seasonal residents from Quebec, France, and French-speaking Caribbean nations.
