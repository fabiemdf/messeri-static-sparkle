About Messeri & Associates

Your dedicated advocate in the complex world of insurance claims

Property-Claim Work Led Personally by David Messeri

Messeri & Associates represents Florida policyholders after property damage and loss. The firm also provides separately administered appraisal, umpire, estimating, and technical consulting services.

David combines close attention to detail, practical negotiation experience, policy analysis, and building-science knowledge to prepare claims supported by the available evidence.

Each policyholder engagement begins with the facts: the property, policy, cause and date of loss, damage documentation, carrier position, prior payments, and applicable deadlines. The goal is a clear, complete presentation—not an unsupported promise of results.

Professional dispute assignments follow a distinct engagement and conflict-check process appropriate to the requested role.

Our Mission & Values

Our mission is to level the playing field between policyholders and insurance companies by providing expert representation that ensures fair and just claim settlements.

We believe in absolute integrity, transparent communication, and relentless advocacy. Every client deserves our full attention and expertise, regardless of the size of their claim.

We are committed to staying at the forefront of industry knowledge and maintaining the highest ethical standards in all our interactions. This commitment has earned us the trust of property owners throughout Florida.
